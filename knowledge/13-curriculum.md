# Curriculum

A progression for teaching the game from first principles to coordinator-level thinking. Each level builds on the one before. Place the learner by asking a few of the check questions, start where they begin to miss, and move on when they can answer that level's questions without notes.

## Level 1: The structure of a play

- **Topics**: the field, downs and distance, the eleven positions on each side, eligible and ineligible players, seven on the line, personnel groupings.
- **Read**: `01-fundamentals.md`.
- **Check**: What is 12 personnel? Why is a tight end with a receiver on the line outside him ineligible? Which defense does 11 personnel usually bring onto the field?
- **Watch for**: name the offensive personnel before each snap of a game.

## Level 2: Formations and what they ask

- **Topics**: quarterback location, distributions (2x2, 3x1, empty), on and off the line, splits, shifts and motion.
- **Read**: `02-offensive-formations.md`.
- **Check**: What does a defender following motion across the formation suggest? What problem does trips create for a defense? Why is play-action better from under center?
- **Watch for**: name the formation, and note what the defense did when a player went in motion.

## Level 3: Gaps, fronts and the run fit

- **Topics**: gap letters, technique numbers, Over and Under, odd fronts, one-gap and two-gap, force, alley and cutback, the box count.
- **Read**: `01-fundamentals.md`, `05-defensive-fronts.md`.
- **Check**: Where is the bubble in an Over front? Who has force in Cover 3 Sky? How many gaps does an offense with one tight end and no fullback create, and how many defenders does a two-high shell leave to fill them?
- **Watch for**: count the box before the snap and predict run or pass.

## Level 4: The run game

- **Topics**: zone and gap schemes, inside and outside zone, duo, power, counter, the back's reads, quarterback reads, constraints.
- **Read**: `03-run-game.md`.
- **Check**: Who does the pulling guard block on power? What does the back read on outside zone? What is the difference between duo and inside zone? Who is unblocked on zone read, and what makes the quarterback keep?
- **Watch for**: find the pullers. No puller usually means zone or duo; one or two means a gap scheme.

## Level 5: Coverage

- **Topics**: MOFO and MOFC, Cover 0 through Cover 6, zone landmarks, man and zone techniques, where each coverage is weak.
- **Read**: `06-coverages.md`.
- **Check**: How many underneath defenders are there in Cover 3 and in Cover 4? Where is the hole shot against Cover 2? What does a corner in press with a single deep safety suggest?
- **Watch for**: call the shell before the snap, then watch the safeties in the first second to see whether it held.

## Level 6: The pass game

- **Topics**: the route tree, timing and drops, concepts as stretches on a defender, progressions, matching concepts to coverages.
- **Read**: `04-pass-game.md`.
- **Check**: Who is the read on slant-flat? Why does smash beat Cover 2 and struggle against Quarters? What does four verticals do to a single deep safety?
- **Watch for**: after a completion, find the defender who had two receivers in his area.

## Level 7: Protection and pressure

- **Topics**: slide, half-slide and big-on-big protections, identifying the Mike, hot routes, fire zones, simulated pressures, Cover 0.
- **Read**: `04-pass-game.md` (protection), `07-pressures.md`.
- **Check**: In a half-slide, who blocks a blitzing linebacker on the man side? What is a simulated pressure, and why does it work with only four rushers? What should the quarterback do against one more rusher than he has blockers?
- **Watch for**: on sacks, decide whether a blocker lost or a rusher came free, and where he came from.

## Level 8: Match coverage and advanced defense

- **Topics**: pattern matching, Quarters rules, Palms, match Cover 3, trips checks, disguise and rotation, spill and box fits, defending RPOs and motion.
- **Read**: `06-coverages.md`, `05-defensive-fronts.md`.
- **Check**: In match Quarters, what does the safety do when #2 goes vertical, and when #2 goes to the flat? What does a Solo check give up? How does a defense make the quarterback's zone-read key wrong?

## Level 9: Systems and play-calling language

- **Topics**: the offensive and defensive trees, how a call is worded, why schemes rose and what answered them.
- **Read**: `08-systems.md`.
- **Check**: How does an Erhardt-Perkins call differ from a Coryell call? Why do wide-zone offenses use condensed formations? What is a two-high shell defense trying to take away, and what does it concede?

## Level 10: Situations and game management

- **Topics**: third down by distance, red zone, two-minute and four-minute, clock and timeouts, fourth-down and two-point decisions, special teams and field position.
- **Read**: `09-situational-football.md`, `10-special-teams.md`.
- **Check**: Up 4 with 1:50 left, the opponent has one timeout and you have first and 10: can you kneel it out? Why go for two when down 8 after a touchdown? What changes for the offense inside the 10?

## Level 11: Thinking like a coordinator

- **Topics**: scouting and tendencies, self-scout, building a plan and a call sheet, scripting, sequencing, in-game adjustment, film study.
- **Read**: `11-game-planning.md`.
- **Capstone**: pick an upcoming game. Scout one team's defense from film or data, choose the core runs and passes to attack it, write the first 15 plays with a reason for each, and prepare the third-down and red-zone calls.

## Teaching methods that work for football

- **Problem first.** Show the look, ask what the offense or defense should do, then name the concept.
- **One conflict at a time.** Reduce each concept to the defender it stresses and his two bad choices.
- **Draw it.** A diagram before terminology.
- **Predict, then reveal.** Before the snap, ask what is coming; afterwards, ask why it worked.
- **Spiral.** Return to earlier levels with harder cases: Cover 3 at Level 5, match Cover 3 at Level 8.
- **Use the simulator** once it exists: call a play against a hidden defensive call, then replay it against the call that beats it.
