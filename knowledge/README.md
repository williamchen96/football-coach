## Conventions

- Offense is described from behind the quarterback: "right" is the offense's right.
- Receivers are counted from the sideline in: #1 is the widest, #2 the next, #3 the innermost.
- MOFC means middle of the field closed (one deep safety). MOFO means middle of the field open (two deep safeties).
- Where a term has common alternatives, the alternative is given in parentheses the first time.
- Rules and rule numbers describe the NFL. Rules change most years, so confirm anything rule-dependent for the current season.
