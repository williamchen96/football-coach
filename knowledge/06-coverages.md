# Coverages

A coverage is named, loosely, for the number of deep defenders. What matters to a coach is three things: how many rush, how many are deep, and who is responsible for each receiver when routes cross zones.

## The families at a glance

| Coverage | Deep | Under | Rush | Shell | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cover 0 | 0 | 0 (all man) | 6+ | No deep safety | Man on every eligible, everyone else rushes |
| Cover 1 | 1 | Man, plus one free helper | 4 or 5 | MOFC | Man with a deep middle safety and usually a "rat" in the hole |
| Cover 2 | 2 | 5 | 4 | MOFO | Two deep halves, corners in the flats |
| Tampa 2 | 2 (plus a runner) | 4 | 4 | MOFO | Cover 2 with the Mike running the deep middle |
| 2-Man | 2 | 5 in man | 4 | MOFO | Man underneath with trail technique, two safeties over the top |
| Cover 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | MOFC | Three deep thirds, four underneath |
| Cover 4 (Quarters) | 4 | 3 | 4 | MOFO | Four deep quarters with pattern-match rules |
| Cover 6 | 3 (quarter, quarter, half) | 4 | 4 | MOFO | Quarters to one side, Cover 2 to the other |

Naming note: some systems call quarter-quarter-half "Cover 6" when the Cover 2 side is to the boundary and "Cover 8" when it is to the field, and some swap them. Nick Saban's "Cover 7" is his family of match-quarters checks.

## Zone landmarks

Underneath zones, from the sideline in: **flat**, **curl**, **hook**, and the **hole** (middle hook). Deep zones are **thirds**, **halves** or **quarters**. A "curl-flat" defender works through the curl to the flat; a "hook-curl" defender does the opposite; a "seam-flat" defender carries a vertical by #2 before settling to the flat.

## Man coverages

### Cover 0

No deep help. Defenders play inside leverage and expect the ball out fast. The offense answers with quick man-beaters, fades and slants, and must account for the unblocked rusher. A busted tackle is a touchdown either way.

### Cover 1

- Corners and the nickel play man, a safety or linebacker takes the tight end, a linebacker takes the back.
- The free safety plays the deep middle. The extra defender is the **rat** (also robber, hole, lurk): he sits in the low middle to cut crossers and spy.
- Variants: **1 Robber** (a safety drops down as the rat), **1 Double** (the extra man doubles a named receiver), **1 Cross** (the rat and safety exchange on deep crossers).
- Weak against: rubs and picks, crossing routes away from the rat, back-shoulder throws, mismatches (a back on a linebacker), quarterback scrambles, since defenders have their backs turned.

### 2-Man

Underneath defenders play **trail** technique: inside and behind the receiver, knowing the safeties are on top. Very good against intermediate routes on passing downs. Weak against the run and draws, quarterback scrambles, rubs, and routes that break away from the trail defender's leverage and away from the safety.

## Zone coverages

### Cover 2

- Corners **squat** in the flats, rerouting #1 to protect the safeties. Safeties play deep halves. Three linebackers or two plus a nickel play hooks and curls.
- Weak against: the **hole shot** on the sideline behind the corner and in front of the safety, around 18 to 22 yards; the deep middle between the safeties; four verticals (two safeties cannot cover four); smash (the corner route behind the squat corner); the run, since two safeties are deep.
- **Tampa 2**: the Mike turns and runs the middle to close the post. This opens the underneath middle in front of him.
- **Cloud** means a corner has the flat; a Cover 2 corner is a cloud corner. **Cover 2 invert** and **trap** versions swap who plays the flat and the half to bait throws.

### Cover 3

- Corners and the free safety play deep thirds. Four underneath: two curl-flat defenders and two hook defenders.
- Named by who rotates down to be the strong curl-flat or the extra hook player:
  - **Sky**: a safety drops to the flat.
  - **Cloud**: the corner takes the flat and a safety takes that deep third.
  - **Buzz**: a safety drops to a hook, a linebacker widens to the flat.
- An eighth defender in the box makes it sound against the run.
- Weak against: **seams** (four verticals put two receivers on the free safety), **curl-flat** (stretching the flat defender), **flood** (three routes through one side's two zones), deep outs and comebacks against bailing corners, and dagger.
- **Match Cover 3 (Rip/Liz, 3 Mod)**: underneath defenders carry verticals by #2 so that four verticals is covered man-to-man after the stems declare. Against trips, **Mable** locks the back-side corner on X and pushes the zone to the three-receiver side.

### Cover 4 (Quarters)

- Four deep defenders, each reading a receiver. In its common match form, the safety reads #2: if #2 goes vertical, the safety takes him; if #2 goes short, the safety robs #1's in-breaking route or doubles him. The corner plays #1 vertical.
- Corner techniques: **MOD** (man only if #1 goes deep, past about 8 yards) and **MEG** (man everywhere he goes).
- **Palms (2-Read, Trap)**: corner and safety both read #2. If #2 breaks to the flat quickly, the corner jumps it and the safety takes #1 over the top. Otherwise it plays as Quarters. It takes away smash and out-breaking quick game.
- Strong against verticals and four-strong concepts; safeties are fast run fitters.
- Weak against: the flats and quick game (only three underneath), play-action that pulls the safeties down (Mills, posts over the top), double moves on the safeties, and crossers that make defenders pass off.

### Cover 6

Quarters to the passing strength or field, Cover 2 to the other side. It handles trips and wide-side threats with quarters while keeping a cloud corner into the boundary. Attack each side with that side's beater: flats and posts to the quarters side, smash and the hole shot to the cloud side.

## Trips checks

A 3x1 set forces a choice. Common answers, by name:

| Check | Idea | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Solo (Poach) | Quarters to trips; the weak safety takes #3 vertical from the far side | The back-side corner is alone on X |
| Special | The trips-side corner locks #1 in man; everyone else plays a two-on-two match on #2 and #3 | Depends on the corner winning |
| Mable | Cover 3 pushed to trips with the back-side corner in man | X one-on-one; back to the weak side on a linebacker |
| Stay zone (spot-drop Cover 3) | No adjustment | Flood and four verticals overload the trips side |
| Rotate to trips | The free safety cheats over | Opens the back-side post and slant |

## Techniques

- **Press**: at the line, disrupting the release. **Off**: 6 to 8 yards deep, eyes on the quarterback and receiver. **Bail**: show press, then turn and run to a deep zone at the snap.
- **Leverage**: inside leverage takes away in-breaking routes and invites the fade and out; outside leverage does the opposite and usually signals inside help.
- **Catch man**: the defender waits at depth and lets the receiver come to him. **Trail**: on the receiver's back hip with help over the top.
- **Spot-drop zone**: defenders run to a landmark and react to the quarterback. Good for interceptions and rallying to tackle; leaves windows.
- **Pattern match**: defenders start in zone and convert to man based on the route distribution. Closes windows; vulnerable to route combinations built to break the rules, and to mental errors.
- **Banjo / switch**: against bunch and stack, defenders take the first receiver in and the first out rather than following through traffic.

## Disguise and rotation

Defenses show one shell and play another. The quarterback confirms after the snap by watching the safeties in his first two steps.

- **Spin down**: two-high before the snap, one safety drops to the box or a hook at the snap, making Cover 3 or Cover 1.
- **Roll to two**: one-high before the snap, the down safety retreats to a half.
- **Invert**: safeties and corners exchange deep and underneath duties.
- A two-high shell before the snap is the most flexible disguise, since every coverage can be reached from it. This is the premise of the Fangio-style defenses.

## Quarterback indicators

| Clue | Suggests |
|---|---|
| One deep safety, corners pressed, defenders following motion | Cover 1 |
| One deep safety, corners off at 7 with eyes inside | Cover 3 |
| Two deep safeties wide, corners squatting at 5 looking in | Cover 2 |
| Two deep safeties at 10 to 12 and flat-footed, corners off | Quarters |
| No deep safety, everyone tight | Cover 0 |
| Linebackers walked out over receivers | Zone |
| A defender running across with motion | Man |

## Beaters, in one table

| Coverage | Where it is weak | Concepts that attack it |
|---|---|---|
| Cover 0 | Everywhere deep; a free rusher | Slants, fades, rubs, screens against the rush, hot throws |
| Cover 1 | Outside one-on-ones, crossers, rubs | Mesh, shallow, Y-Cross, bunch rubs, back-shoulder fade, option routes |
| 2-Man | Run, scramble, breaks away from leverage | Draws, mesh, option routes, back out of the backfield |
| Cover 2 | Hole shot, deep middle, run | Smash, four verticals with a bender, flood, post between the safeties |
| Tampa 2 | Underneath middle, hole shot | Dig and drag under the Mike, smash, check-downs |
| Cover 3 | Seams, flats, intermediate outside | Four verticals, curl-flat, flood, dagger, stick, comebacks |
| Cover 4 | Flats, quick game, play-action posts | Stick, spacing, slant-flat, Mills, double moves, run game at safeties |
| Cover 6 | The seam between the two halves of the call | The Cover 2 beater on one side and the Cover 4 beater on the other |
